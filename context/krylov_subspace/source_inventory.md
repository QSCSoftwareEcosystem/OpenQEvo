## Legacy reference cleanup and limits

The prior prose summary used the wrong Stair title/coauthor (Alenciar) and
unsupported universal convergence/hardware statements. The inspected primary
record identifies Huang; the summary now delegates to the structured chain.
Other old references (Motta, Parrish, Cortes, Eom, Piccinelli) remain research
leads until individually validated for the claimed role; they are not needed
for these three promoted concepts. The Piccinelli conversion is separately
inventoried in the [legacy qDRIFT collection](../qdrift/source_inventory.md).
No claim here establishes a sparse, quantum, distributed, or imaginary-time
Krylov implementation in OpenQEvo. Acquisition of full text with equation-level
locators and expert assessment of bounds remain follow-up tasks.
